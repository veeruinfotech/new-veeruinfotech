Who We Are

Veeru Infotech is a one-stop shop for all your computer, laptop, CCTV, and IT accessory needs. Based in Kudasan, Gandhinagar, we have built a reputation for honest pricing, expert technicians, and reliable after-sales support.

Our mission is simple: "Your Technology, Our Priority." We treat every customer like family and ensure your devices work flawlessly.
